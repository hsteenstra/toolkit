      Common questions

      "But what about…"

      The worries we hear most from schools, and our answers.

      I've never coded. Can I really lead this?

      Yes. Every lesson is step by step, and the Teacher Workbook has the answers. The 5-Day Python Textbook was written for teachers who have never coded. Learning alongside your students is a great lesson in itself.

      How much does it cost?

      Nothing. Every online tool is free. If you want a printed guide for a camp, the 5-Day Python Textbook is $10 as a PDF or $20 printed (plus shipping), but you don't need it to get started.

      Our school blocks a lot of websites.

      There's nothing to download. Ask your IT team to make sure codeforme.org, hsteenstra.github.io and cdn.jsdelivr.net load on student devices. The last one is how the Python tools run Python inside the browser.

      Do students need accounts or email addresses?

      No. Students just open a link and start. There's nothing to sign up for.

      My students are at very different levels.

      Pick a starting point by what students have done before, not by their age. Pair students up so one types and one helps, and let fast finishers move on to the next tool in your plan or help a classmate.

      Can Code For ME help us get started?

      We'd love to. Email codeformaine@gmail.com and tell us about your school.

      Want a hand planning?

      We're students and teachers from Bath, Maine. Tell us what you're thinking and we'll help you get started.
